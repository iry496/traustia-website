# Traustia Evidence Disc

The Traustia Evidence Disc is a Great Gray Owl constructed from three evidence layers, a central `T`, and one verified evidence node.

## Production assets

- `public/brand/traustia-evidence-disc.svg` — master two-color mark.
- `public/brand/traustia-evidence-disc-micro.svg` — simplified mark for sizes below 32 px.
- `public/brand/traustia-evidence-disc-black.svg` — one-color black trademark artwork.
- `public/brand/traustia-evidence-disc-white.svg` — one-color white trademark artwork.
- `public/favicon.svg` — reversed micro mark on the Traustia navy field.

## Canonical colors

- Traustia Navy: `#07131F`
- Evidence Teal: `#35C5B4`
- Evidence White: `#F6F8F5`

## Usage rules

- Use the master mark at 32 px and above.
- Use the micro mark below 32 px.
- Preserve clear space equal to the teal node diameter on every side.
- Do not move the evidence node, close the ring breaks, add ear tufts, or place the mark inside a shield.
- Use only the black or white artwork for trademark filings and single-ink reproduction.

## Motion rules

Website motion may rotate the evidence rings by no more than 12 degrees and pulse the evidence node. The owl face and central `T` must remain stable. Motion must be disabled when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is active.
